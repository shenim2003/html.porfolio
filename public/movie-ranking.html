THE BEST MOVIES ACCORDING TO SHEHAN

My best five movies all the time

Lord of the ring

The Lord of the Rings is an epic high-fantasy novel written by J. R. R. Tolkien. Set in the fictional world of Middle-earth, the story follows the young hobbit Frodo Baggins on a perilous quest to destroy the One Ring, a powerful and corrupting artifact created by the Dark Lord Sauron.

Pirates of the Caribbean

Pirates of the Caribbean is a blockbuster Disney media franchise that spans a hit film series, theme park attractions, novels, and video games. It follows the swashbuckling and supernatural adventures of eccentric pirate Captain Jack Sparrow, noble Will Turner, and spirited Elizabeth Swann across the high seas.

Transformers

Transformers: Dark of the Moon (2011) is the third installment in the live-action sci-fi action film series directed by Michael Bay. It follows the Autobots and Sam Witwicky as they race the Decepticons to a crashed Cybertronian spacecraft on the Moon, the discovery of which sparks a devastating global invasion.

Harry Potter

Harry Potter is the fictional protagonist of J.K. Rowling’s fantasy series. An orphaned wizard, he is famously known as "The Boy Who Lived" for surviving the killing curse of the dark wizard Lord Voldemort as an infant. He attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he fights to defeat Voldemort.

Avengers: Endgame

The Avengers are an all-star superhero team known as "Earth's Mightiest Heroes," tasked with protecting the world from extraterrestrial, supernatural, and domestic threats. Created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby for Marvel Comics in 1963, the concept features an ever-changing roster of Marvel's most popular heroes
